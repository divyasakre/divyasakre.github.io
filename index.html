About me

An IT engineer raised around instrumentation.

My dad's background is in instrumentation and electrical engineering. I chose IT, partly because it was booming and partly because I suspected his world and mine would meet someday. They did.

At Ketsol in Pune I started as a database developer, building batch, alarm and audit reports for a pharma plant and putting them on the SCADA screen so operators didn't have to dig through paper. When Ketsol started building its own software, I moved to full stack. I connected software to PLCs, VFDs, scanners and cameras: sending recipes to machines, getting alerts back, and bringing shop-floor data into one place.

Watching my managers bring clients, plans and teams together, I knew I wanted to lead that work one day. That's why I came to UT Dallas for an MS in Information Technology and Management. Now I'm a software engineer at CauseFleet, building the data backbone of an analytics platform.

What I'm looking for: MES, manufacturing IT, full stack and AI roles where software meets real machines and real operations.
