KVision platform

KVision is Ketsol's industrial computer vision platform. I worked directly with 3 manufacturing clients:

  • Collected video and images on the plant floor.
  • Trained YOLO models for object, defect (such as scratches), color and text (product name) detection.
  • Deployed the models at client sites, reaching 70%+ accuracy at 120 FPS.
  • Built the platform's database, CRUD APIs and UI, with a multi-threaded camera pipeline.

One photo per pipe

A steel pipe manufacturer needed exactly one image of every pipe. I built an OpenCV system that uses each pipe's dwell time in the camera area to capture it once and skip duplicates, with no extra sensor.

Pass/fail on the edge

For an industrial clamp manufacturer, I built a vision service on NVIDIA Jetson that classifies parts as pass or fail from a live camera feed and logs the results.
